I'm a data scientist and quantitative researcher based in San Francisco. My work sits at the intersection of survey research, measurement, and applied artificial intelligence.

I have built machine-learning pipelines, predictive models, attribution dashboards, and data-quality systems supporting a survey panel with more than 230,000 members and millions of responses. Previously, I worked as a researcher at the [Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en), as chief technology officer at the Island Ark Project Foundation, and as a data strategy consultant at 180 Degrees Consulting.

My academic research examines how climate adaptation programs shape individual behavior and political attitudes in Indonesia. I use experimental and quasi-experimental methods with large-scale survey data from more than 45,000 respondents across more than 30 countries. My work has been published in *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* and *International Affairs*, and has been funded by Meta, the Russell Sage Foundation, and UC Santa Barbara.

I also take on consulting projects for research and insights teams, particularly around survey design, data quality, quantitative analysis, and research systems. [See how I can help.](/services/)
